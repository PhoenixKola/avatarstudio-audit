AvatarStudio Terms of Service

Effective date: June 2026

1. Overview

AvatarStudio is a local/internal video creation tool that helps users create short-form videos using scripts, narration, captions, generated visuals, and rendered video assets.

AvatarStudio may include an optional YouTube upload feature that allows an authenticated user to upload a selected finished video to their own connected YouTube channel.

2. Local/Internal Use

AvatarStudio is currently intended for local/internal use. The application itself is not publicly distributed as a hosted consumer service. The public website exists to provide documentation, privacy information, and terms for review purposes.

3. YouTube Channel Connection

Users may connect a YouTube channel through Google OAuth. AvatarStudio only accesses YouTube functionality after the user grants permission through Google’s authorization flow.

Users can disconnect their YouTube channel from AvatarStudio at any time from the Channels page. Users may also revoke access from their Google Account permissions page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions .

4. YouTube Uploads

AvatarStudio uploads videos only when the user manually selects a rendered video and confirms the upload action. The app does not upload videos automatically without user action.

When uploading, the user controls metadata such as title, description, tags, privacy status, made-for-kids setting, altered or synthetic media disclosure, and optional caption track upload.

5. YouTube API Services

AvatarStudio uses YouTube API Services only to provide user-requested functionality, including identifying the authenticated channel, uploading selected videos, and optionally uploading caption tracks.

Use of YouTube through AvatarStudio is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service , the Google Privacy Policy , and applicable YouTube policies.

6. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for the videos, scripts, images, audio, captions, metadata, and other content they create, render, or upload using AvatarStudio.

Users must ensure that uploaded videos comply with YouTube policies, copyright rules, privacy laws, and any other applicable laws or platform requirements.

7. AI-Generated and Altered Content

AvatarStudio may help create AI-generated scripts, images, captions, narration, or video assets. Users are responsible for reviewing generated content before uploading it.

When content contains altered or synthetic media, users are responsible for using the appropriate disclosure settings during upload.

8. Captions

AvatarStudio may support burned-in captions and optional YouTube caption track uploads. Users are responsible for reviewing caption accuracy before rendering or uploading videos.

9. No Unauthorized Access

AvatarStudio does not access YouTube channels without user authorization. The app does not scrape YouTube, does not access third-party channels without consent, and does not sell YouTube user data.

10. Changes to the Service

AvatarStudio may be updated over time to improve video creation, rendering, YouTube upload workflows, caption handling, and channel management features.

11. Disclaimer

AvatarStudio is provided as a local/internal tool. The user is responsible for reviewing all generated content and uploaded metadata before publishing content on YouTube or any other platform.
